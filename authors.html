Guidelines for Authors

1. General Requirements

The Academic Journal of Science and Progress accepts original research papers that have not been previously published and are not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere.

  • Language of Publication: Uzbek, Russian, or English.
  • Length: From 6 to 15 pages.
  • Originality (Anti-Plagiarism): A minimum similarity threshold of 80% is required. All submissions undergo automated screening.

2. Manuscript Formatting and Layout

  • File Format: MS Word (.docx).
  • Maximum File Size: 3MB.
  • Font and Size: Times New Roman, 12 pt.
  • Line Spacing: 1.15 or 1.5.
  • Margins: 2 cm on all sides.
  • Formulas and Tables: Equations must be inserted using MS Equation / MathType; tables and figures must be consecutively numbered and captioned.

3. Manuscript Structure

  1. UDC Index (placed in the upper left corner of the page).
  2. Article Title (capitalized, concise, and reflective of the content).
  3. Author Information: Full Name, academic degree/title, institutional affiliation, ORCID iD, and corresponding author's email address.
  4. Abstract: 150–250 words outlining the research objective, methodology, and primary findings.
  5. Keywords: 5–8 key terms or phrases.
  6. Main Body (following the IMRAD format):
    • Introduction — contextual background, relevance, and objectives of the study.
    • Materials and Methods — methodological framework and research techniques.
    • Results — empirical data and analytical findings.
    • Discussion and Conclusion — interpretation of results and final conclusions.
  7. References: Inclusion of DOI links for cited sources is mandatory.

4. Peer Review Process

All submitted manuscripts undergo a double-blind peer review process (Blind Review) conducted by subject matter experts. Based on the review outcomes, manuscripts are either accepted for publication, returned for revision, or rejected.

Publication Ethics

The Academic Journal of Science and Progress strictly adheres to the core principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and internationally recognized academic publishing standards.

  • Authorship and Contributorship: Authorship must be limited to those who have made a significant contribution to the conception, design, execution, or interpretation of the reported study. Honorary authorship or the omission of genuine contributors is strictly prohibited.
  • Plagiarism and Fabrication: Any form of plagiarism, unauthorized appropriation of others' ideas without proper citation, and data fabrication or falsification are strictly forbidden. All submissions are screened using automated anti-plagiarism detection tools.
  • Duplicate Submission: Submitting manuscripts that have been previously published or are concurrently under consideration in other scholarly journals is impermissible.
  • Conflict of Interest: Authors must disclose any financial, personal, or organizational conflicts of interest that could inappropriately influence or bias their research outcomes or interpretation.
  • Peer Review Integrity: Reviewers and editorial board members ensure the confidentiality and objective evaluation of manuscripts through a Double-Blind Peer Review process. Reviewers are prohibited from utilizing manuscript data for personal advantage.
  • Retraction Policy: In the event of significant errors or breaches of publication ethics in published articles, the editorial board reserves the right to issue retractions or corrections in accordance with COPE guidelines.
