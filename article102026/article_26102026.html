WATER QUALITY OF SURFACE WATER BODIES IN BUKHARA REGION

Author: U.A.Sodikova, I.A.Usmanov, M.Sh.Abdiyeva
Specialty: Hydraulics and Engineering Hydrology
Institution: ISMITI
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23702867
Views: ...
Abstract
This article analyzes the hydrochemical quality indicators of the waters of Tuzkon Lake, South Kogon Collector, Zarafshan River, and Amu-Bukhara Canal in the Bukhara region for the years 2021-2024 from the perspective of drinking water quality. The study examined multi-year changes in mineralization, chloride, sulfate, ammonium, and nitrite concentrations in the water. A strong positive correlation was found between mineralization and the concentration of chlorides and sulfates. The obtained results serve as a scientific basis for assessing water quality change trends and conducting regular environmental monitoring in water bodies.
