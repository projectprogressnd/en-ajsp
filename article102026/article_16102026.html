IMPACT OF IRRIGATION TECHNOLOGIES ON THE MELIORATIVE CONDITION OF LANDS

Author: O‘razkeldiyev Abduvoxid Baxtiyarovich
Specialty: Hydraulics and Engineering Hydrology
Institution: SRIIWP
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23558126
Views: ...
Abstract
This paper presents the results of field experiments based on ISMITI methods aimed at reducing the formation of collector-drainage waters and their outflow beyond the regional boundary in the Khorezm region, by introducing scientifically grounded irrigation regimes using water-saving drip irrigation for cotton, taking into account the soil-climatic conditions and land meliorative state of the Khorezm oasis under global climate change and water scarcity.
