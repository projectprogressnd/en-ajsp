Payment Procedure

1. Stages Prior to Payment

The publication of scientific articles in the Academic Journal of Science and Progress is governed by stringent and transparent academic standards. The publication workflow comprises the following sequential phases:

  1. Manuscript Submission: Authors submit their manuscripts, meticulously formatted in accordance with the specifications delineated in the For Authors section via the official web portal.
  2. Editorial Screening and Peer Review: Submitted contributions undergo rigorous preliminary evaluation for journal compliance, followed by a rigorous double-blind peer review process.
  3. Final Acceptance: A manuscript receives formal authorization for publication exclusively upon securing a favorable evaluation and endorsement from the Editorial Board.

2. Acquisition of a Secure Payment Credential

Please be advised that unsolicited or advance remittances are strictly impermissible.

Upon the definitive ratification of your manuscript by the Editorial Board and its demonstrated alignment with all requisite criteria, an encrypted, dedicated payment link will be transmitted directly to your registered electronic mail address.

Important Notice

Subsequent to the verification of payment, your article will be scheduled for incorporation into the forthcoming issue and formally promulgated alongside a designated Digital Object Identifier (DOI). Should any queries arise concerning the financial transaction or data security, please feel free to communicate with our editorial office via the Contact section.
