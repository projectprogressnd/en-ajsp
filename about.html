About the Journal & Aims

Academic Journal of Science and Progress is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to publishing innovative research across a broad spectrum of scientific and technical disciplines. The journal serves as a premier platform for researchers, scientists, and engineers to share knowledge that contributes to theoretical and practical advancements, as well as technological progress.

Scientific advancement is deeply intertwined with technological innovation, and interdisciplinary research is becoming increasingly critical in addressing complex global challenges. Achievements in engineering, computer science, materials science, environmental studies, and applied sciences continue to transform industries, research organizations, and modern society. The journal welcomes submissions that present novel scientific ideas, technological solutions, and results of interdisciplinary collaboration that drive scientific progress and technological development forward.

Academic Journal of Science and Progress accepts peer-reviewed research papers specializing in natural sciences, engineering, computer science, materials science, environmental sciences, and emerging technological fields. By providing open access to high-quality peer-reviewed research, the journal promotes the global dissemination of scientific knowledge and fosters active collaboration among scholars and professionals across diverse academic domains.
The "Academic Journal of Science and Progress" operates as an editorial board established within the structure of LLC "Project Progress" and does not constitute a separate legal entity. All financial operations are conducted by the founder of LLC "Project Progress".
