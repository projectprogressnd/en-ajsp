About the Journal and Aims

Academic Journal of Science and Progress is an international peer-reviewed open-access journal dedicated to publishing innovative research across a wide range of scientific and technical fields. The journal serves as a platform for researchers, scientists, and engineers to share knowledge that contributes to scientific discoveries and technological development in both theoretical and practical domains.

Scientific progress is closely intertwined with technological innovation, and interdisciplinary research is becoming increasingly important in solving complex global challenges. Advancements in engineering, computer science, materials science, environmental studies, and applied sciences continue to transform industries, research institutions, and modern society. The journal welcomes publications that present novel scientific ideas, technological solutions, and results of interdisciplinary collaboration contributing to scientific progress and technological development.

Academic Journal of Science and Progress accepts research works specializing in natural sciences, engineering, computer science, materials science, environmental sciences, and prospective technological areas. By providing open access to high-quality peer-reviewed research, the journal promotes the global dissemination of scientific knowledge and encourages cooperation among scholars and professionals operating in various scientific fields.
The "Academic Journal of Science and Progress" is not a legal entity and operates as an editorial board established within the structure of LLC "Project Progress". All financial transactions are conducted by the founder of LLC "Project Progress".
